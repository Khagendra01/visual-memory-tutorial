One-minute design exercise

Choose keys, a wallet, or a remote control. Describe an enrollment procedure, one likely recognition failure, and the message the application should display when it cannot confidently answer. Then explain which data you would retain and which data you would delete.

Example response: a remote control

Enroll front and back views under two lighting conditions and give the remote a user-selected name. Similar remotes may produce ambiguous appearance matches. Show the enrolled examples and ask the user to choose before retrieving history. Keep a small number of evidence crops, dated positions, and map identifiers. Delete rejected frames and provide an erase-history control. If alignment fails, show “Last seen beside the sofa at 8:10. Scan the room to restore directional guidance.”
